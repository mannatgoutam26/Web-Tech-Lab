About the College

Government college of Engineering & Technology was established in 1994. The college offers courses in Computer Science, Electronics, Mechanical,Electrical and Civil Engineering. We have experienced teachers, a big library, well equipped labs and a green campus.

Every year our students take part in technical fests, sports events and cultural programs.
